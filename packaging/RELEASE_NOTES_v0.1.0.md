The first QRUDO release: your hand becomes the remote.

**Control your computer by gesture.** Play and pause, seek, volume,
brightness — with a fist, a palm, two fingers — from your chair or
across the room.

**Teach it your own gestures.** Record any shape your hand can make —
one hand or two — and tie it to anything: open a folder, launch or
quit or hide an app, visit a site, press keys, run a chain. Set-up
takes a typed sentence; the gesture then works offline, forever.

**Two-hand gestures.** Both palms, both fists, both points — each a
gesture of its own, plus any pair of shapes you invent.

**Built to stay alive.** A crash supervisor restarts the engine within
a second; permissions survive updates; the detection floor is pinned
by tests that run on macOS and Windows for every change.

**Install (macOS):** unzip, drag QRUDO.app to Applications, launch,
allow the camera, enable Accessibility for media keys — once each.
